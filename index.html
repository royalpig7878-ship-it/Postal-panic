POSTAL PANIC

Deliver the mail through endless neighborhoods — from sunny suburbs to autumn streets, dusk, snow and night. Dodge dogs, geese, grumpy neighbors and rock-dropping birds, then spend your coins on upgrades and fresh style for you and your truck.

5 biomes · 10 levels each · endless · v2

How to Play

Load packages at the 🚚 truck, deliver to every 🏠 mailbox, then return to the truck to finish the level. Beat a level to advance — they get longer and harder forever. Throw junk mail (limited ✉️ ammo) at enemies; grab glowing mail-sacks to restock. Watch the sky: birds drop rocks — dodge their shadow!

Mind the gaps! 🌉 walk the bridge, ride the moving platform, or press Jump near a 🪢 rope to swing across. Jump onto raised ground and floating platforms.
