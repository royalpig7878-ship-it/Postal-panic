POSTAL PANIC

Deliver the mail through endless neighborhoods — from sunny suburbs to autumn streets, dusk, snow, night and the neon future city. Dodge dogs, geese, grumpy neighbors and rock-dropping birds, then spend your coins on upgrades and fresh style for you and your truck.

6 neighborhoods · 10 levels + a boss each · endless · v3

How to Play

Move ← → / A D   Sprint Shift (touch: tap 💨 to toggle)
Jump Space / W (press again in the air to double jump; 🦘 Spring Soles in the shop make it stronger)   Throw F
Interact E   Pause Esc / P   Mute M

Load packages at the 🚚 truck, deliver to every 🏠 mailbox, then return to the truck to finish the level. Beat a level to advance — they get longer and harder forever. Throw junk mail (limited ✉️ ammo) at enemies; grab glowing mail-sacks to restock. Each neighborhood adds its own troublemaker: 🐿 acorn-throwing squirrels (Autumn), 🦝 package-stealing raccoons (Dusk), ☃️ snowball kids and slippery ice (Snow), possums and swooping bats (Night), and 🤖 armored robot dogs (Neo City). Need a breather? Jump on the 🚚 truck roof: dogs, geese and neighbors can't reach you there. Watch the sky: birds drop rocks — dodge their shadow!

After every neighborhood's 10th level comes a ⚔ boss fight: hit the boss with mail and dodge its attacks. Your progress, coins and upgrades are saved. Use 🗺 Levels to replay any level you've unlocked (replays pay 25% coins). Every 5th level is an 🏢 apartment building: press E at the front door to go in, deliver to each numbered door, and use the 🛗 elevator to change floors.

🏆 Earn achievements for deliveries, knockouts, flawless shifts, bosses and more. Each one pays bonus coins the moment you unlock it.

Hide under platforms or the truck when birds drop rocks: anything falling stops on top of them. Mind the gaps! 🌉 walk the bridge, ride the moving platform, or press Jump near a 🪢 rope to swing across. Jump onto raised ground and floating platforms.
